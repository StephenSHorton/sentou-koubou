          Flipbook fallback

          Use when you don’t have a bone rig yet — or for VFX overlays. BaseLib can also drive Godot AnimatedSprite2D from frame sequences.

          Spine Player (official runtime)

          Drop a Spine export set: *.json or *.skel + *.atlas + texture PNG(s). Version should match runtime 4.2.x (change CDN if you export with another Spine version).

          Why Spine Player?

          STS2 uses Spine under the hood (MegaSpine). Previewing real exports here is the closest in-browser check before packing into the mod.
